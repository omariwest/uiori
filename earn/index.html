Getting started

Three simple steps to start earning

  1. 1

    Review and choose

    UIORI shows the participation details before you continue.

  2. 2

    Complete the Mellowtel setup

    On a desktop, continue to Mellowtel, install its companion extension, and choose to participate.

  3. 3

    Track your progress

    When you return, UIORI verifies Mellowtel in this browser automatically. Then track points, streaks, milestones, and credited rewards from your dashboard.

Your choice

You stay in control

  • Creating an account does not start earning.
  • You choose when earning is active.
  • You can turn earning off whenever you want.
  • Only credited earnings count as money.

UIORI uses Mellowtel's separate companion-extension flow. Your UIORI account alone never starts bandwidth sharing.

Public-web requests Approved public-web requests may be routed through your connection, so destination sites can see your public IP as the request source.
Separate consent & control Mellowtel presents its own consent step and provides independent controls so you can opt out later.
UIORI rewards Reward amounts can vary and follow UIORI's Rewards Policy. Credited rewards appear in your available balance.
