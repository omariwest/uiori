FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about buying Telegram advertising through UIORI.

UIORI is an independent marketplace for discovering, booking, funding, and tracking sponsored placements on Telegram channels.

No. UIORI uses Telegram as distribution infrastructure but is not Telegram and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Telegram unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Browse the marketplace, choose eligible channels, create your sponsored-post draft and preferred schedule, then submit it for review. Funding happens only after approval.

UIORI is designed to show factual marketplace data such as subscribers, recent or median reach, ERR, placement formats, and prices when those measurements actually exist. Missing data is not invented.

A campaign is funded only after UIORI receives the required final payment confirmation from the applicable payment process. Creating an invoice or sending an unconfirmed transaction is not enough.

On eligible inventory, the order may include a minimum Telegram-reported post-view floor within a defined delivery window. Telegram's view counter is not represented as verified unique humans.

UIORI reviews the campaign record and delivery evidence. Depending on the order, the remedy may be correction, rescheduling, a make-good, credit, or refund under the Refund & Delivery Protection Policy.

No. Unless an order explicitly says otherwise, UIORI does not guarantee clicks, conversions, sales, subscriber growth, or other advertiser business outcomes.

UIORI rejects unsafe, deceptive, unlawful, phishing, wallet-draining, impersonation, guaranteed-profit, fake-airdrop, and other prohibited campaigns described in the Advertising Policy.

Partner onboarding is planned for a later phase. The first marketplace inventory may be UIORI-operated while the channel-owner system is developed and verified.

Use the Contact page or email support@uiori.com for account, campaign, billing, moderation, or delivery questions.
