Safety & control

You decide when earning starts.

Creating an account never starts bandwidth sharing. You activate earning deliberately and can stop whenever you want.

Your control

Your earning controls stay visible.

UIORI keeps the important choices simple: start when you are ready, see your status, and stop when you want.

  • You choose when earning starts

    Nothing activates just because you created an account.

  • Your status stays visible

    The dashboard clearly shows whether earning is active.

  • You can stop anytime

    Mellowtel provides independent participation controls, and stopping participation does not remove your UIORI account.

Clear by design

Progress, money, and controls stay separate.

The dashboard separates the states that matter so you can understand your account at a glance.

Earning controls

UIORI keeps your earning state easy to understand.

  • Current earning status
  • Start and stop controls
  • Connection state
  • No automatic activation

Reward controls

Your progress and money stay clearly separated.

  • Contribution Points, streaks, levels, and milestones
  • Credited earnings balance
  • Withdrawal eligibility

The earning setup shows the resource use and public-IP disclosure before it opens Mellowtel's separate consent flow. You can also manage participation independently through Mellowtel user controls.

UIORI safeguards

Built to protect fair rewards.

Account checks and reward rules help protect fair progress and withdrawals while keeping the experience simple.

Account integrity

Verification may be required, and duplicate or abusive accounts can be restricted.

Reward integrity

Rewards are based on eligible activity and cannot be created by repeated clicking or refreshing.

Withdrawal review

A withdrawal may be reviewed when account activity needs verification.
