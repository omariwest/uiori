Affiliate Disclosure.

How uiori may earn money, how links are labeled, and how we protect users from confused payout claims.

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Plain rule: uiori may earn money when users click referral, affiliate, CPA, advertising, or sponsored links. This never means uiori pays users directly, guarantees earnings, or controls third party platform approvals.

How uiori may earn money

Some links on uiori may be referral or affiliate links. If you join a third party platform or complete a qualifying action through one of those links, uiori may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

uiori may also earn from display ads, CPA offers, content locker sponsors for optional bonus files, and clearly labeled sponsored placements.

Type What it means User impact
Referral link A platform may reward uiori if you sign up through our link. No extra cost from uiori.
Affiliate link uiori may earn a commission for a qualified signup, purchase, or action. The platform still controls eligibility and payment.
CPA offer A sponsor may pay uiori when a user completes a defined action. Optional; not required to use the main site.

Display ads Ad networks may show ads on pages if enabled. Ads may use cookies or similar technologies.

Editorial standards

Commission potential should not remove important warnings. uiori pages should still explain country limits, payout methods, minimum withdrawal notes, task difficulty, platform risks, and alternatives when they are useful.

We prefer cautious wording such as “may be available,” “check current eligibility,” and “availability can change” because MMO platforms often vary by country, profile, and time.

Sponsored placements

Sponsored listings, sponsored guides, or paid placements should be identified clearly near the placement. A sponsored placement is not an endorsement that the platform will work for every user, country, payout method, or profile.

Optional downloads and lockers

uiori may offer bonus resources such as trackers, checklists, or PDF guides. If a sponsor action or content locker is used, it should be presented as an optional way to unlock a bonus file, not as an earning task from uiori.

Third party responsibility

uiori is a research guide, not an employer, wallet, payment processor, or earning platform. Third party platforms control eligibility, tasks, approvals, payment, disputes, and account rules. Always read the official platform rules before creating an account or spending time on tasks.
