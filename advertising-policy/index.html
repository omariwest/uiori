Advertising Policy

Campaigns must be truthful, safe, legally compliant, and suitable for the Telegram audiences they are submitted to.

Last updated 16 November 2026

1Purpose

This policy applies to every campaign submitted to UIORI. Approval is based on the creative, destination, product, target audience, applicable law, and the rules of the selected Telegram inventory.

Submitting or funding a campaign does not guarantee publication.

2Core advertising standard

Advertising must be truthful, clearly attributable, safe to open, and consistent with the destination page. Material conditions, costs, eligibility restrictions, and risks must not be hidden or contradicted by the landing page.

Sponsored placements may be labeled Sponsored or with another clear advertising disclosure.

3Prohibited advertising

  • Phishing, malware, wallet drainers, malicious downloads, credential theft, seed phrase or private key requests.
  • Impersonation, fake support accounts, fake endorsements, counterfeit identities, or misleading claims of being official.
  • Guaranteed profit, guaranteed token appreciation, fabricated returns, pump and dump schemes, or deceptive investment claims.
  • Fake giveaways, fake airdrops, hidden deposit requirements, or deceptive pay to withdraw schemes.
  • Illegal goods or services, sanctions evasion, money laundering facilitation, stolen goods, or other unlawful activity.
  • Gambling, betting, casino, or similar wagering offers at launch.
  • Pornographic content or sexual services.
  • Content that infringes intellectual property or uses material without the necessary rights.
  • False scarcity, fabricated testimonials, fake metrics, or material claims that cannot be substantiated.

4Crypto and financial advertising

Crypto, Web3, wallet, exchange, stablecoin, token, airdrop, and financial service advertising receives enhanced review. UIORI may require evidence of the project's identity, official domain, product status, regional availability, licensing or authorization where applicable, risk disclosures, token or reward terms, and the absence of prohibited wallet permissions.

Ads must not present speculative assets as guaranteed income or hide meaningful financial risk.

5Airdrops, quests, and reward campaigns

Advertisers must clearly disclose material participation requirements, including deposit or purchase requirements, KYC where known, regional restrictions, deadlines, network fees, and whether rewards are points, tokens, NFTs, or not yet defined.

Future reward or token value must not be represented as guaranteed.

6Landing pages and links

Destination links must use secure HTTPS where technically applicable, must not unexpectedly redirect to a materially different offer, and must remain safe and functional during the campaign. UIORI may reject shortened, cloaked, or redirecting links when they prevent meaningful review.

7Targeting and local rules

Advertisers are responsible for ensuring their product and advertising are lawful for the countries or audiences they target. UIORI may limit a campaign to particular regions or reject it where legal or platform risk is unclear.

8Creative quality

Creative should be understandable, reasonably professional, and suitable for the selected audience. Excessive capitalization, deceptive buttons, misleading urgency, spam like repetition, or unrelated hashtags may be rejected or edited only with advertiser approval.

9Review and enforcement

UIORI may approve, reject, request changes, pause, or remove an advertisement. A campaign that becomes unsafe or misleading after approval may be stopped even after publication.

Advertisers may contact support@uiori.com if they believe a moderation decision was made in error.
