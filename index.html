Does uiori pay users directly?

No. uiori helps you research third-party platforms. Any task, approval, balance, or payout happens inside the platform you join, not inside uiori.

Are earnings guaranteed?

No. Country access, profile fit, task supply, approval, and payment rules can change. Test one small route before spending serious time.

Why does country matter so much?

The same platform can show different tasks, payout methods, and limits by country. That is why uiori starts with country first.

What should a beginner do first?

Pick one country page, choose one payout method you can receive, and try one low-risk platform until you confirm a real withdrawal route.

Do referral links affect the guidance?

Some links may earn uiori a commission, but warnings should stay visible. A useful page explains limits before asking for a click.
