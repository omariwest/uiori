How it works

From signup to visible progress.

Full walkthrough

Step 1

Create or sign in to your UIORI account.

Use your UIORI account to continue to earning setup.

Step 2

Open Earn and start the setup.

UIORI shows the participation details first. Nothing starts just because you have an account.

Step 3

Add the Mellowtel extension.

UIORI takes you to the support page, then the extension install page. Add the extension to continue the guided setup.

Step 4

Turn support on when you are ready.

The switch is yours. When it is on, a small part of your unused internet connection can be used while you browse.

Step 5

Stop support whenever you want.

Open Mellowtel settings and turn support off whenever you want to stop participating.
