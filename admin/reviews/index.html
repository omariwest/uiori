Review process

Verify before changing account state

A review should be resolved from the available account, earning, reward, and withdrawal records rather than from a single signal alone.

  • Confirm the account and affected activity.
  • Check related reward or withdrawal records.
  • Document the reason for any account action.
  • Avoid changing credited balances without supporting records.

Resolved reviews remain available in the administrative history together with the action that closed them.
