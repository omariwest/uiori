Privacy

Privacy Policy.

What uiori may collect, why it is used, how third party links work, and how to contact us about privacy questions.

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Simple version: uiori is a content site. We do not run user accounts, wallets, or payouts. We may collect basic site analytics, email messages you send us, affiliate tracking data, and advertising or cookie data if those tools are enabled.

Information we may collect

uiori only needs limited information to operate a useful content site. The exact data collected depends on which analytics, advertising, email, affiliate, or security tools are active.

Information Examples Why it may be used
Site usage data Pages viewed, referring page, device type, browser, approximate country. Improve content, fix pages, understand what users need.
Contact information Your email address and message when you contact us. Reply to corrections, reports, privacy requests, or partnerships.
Affiliate data Click IDs, referral IDs, conversion status, or similar tracking data. Track commissions and understand which links are useful.
Cookie and ad data Cookies, local storage, ad identifiers, IP-related signals, or web beacons. Analytics, advertising, fraud prevention, frequency capping, or measurement.
Security data IP address, request logs, error logs, or suspicious activity signals. Protect the site, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

Information uiori does not ask for

uiori does not ask users to create a wallet, upload identity documents, send payment details, share platform passwords, or complete tasks for uiori in exchange for promised payouts.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

uiori may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, affiliate tracking, advertising, security, and site improvement. Third party vendors may place or read cookies, use web beacons, or use similar technologies when ads, analytics, or affiliate tools are enabled.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Some regions may require additional consent options for certain cookies or personalized advertising; if those tools are added, uiori should use an appropriate consent solution.

Third party links and platforms

When you click a link to a platform, ad, affiliate partner, or sponsor, you leave uiori. That third party may collect information under its own privacy policy. uiori does not control third party account creation, identity checks, task tracking, payment systems, cookies, or support decisions.

How we may use information

  • Improve pages, guides, platform notes, and country coverage.
  • Respond to emails, corrections, privacy requests, or reports.
  • Measure link performance, affiliate commissions, and sponsor results.
  • Protect the site from spam, abuse, technical errors, and security issues.
  • Comply with legal, tax, platform, or advertising obligations when required.

How information may be shared

uiori may share limited information with service providers that help operate the site, such as hosting, analytics, email, advertising, affiliate tracking, security, or spam protection providers. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the site and its users.

Data retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, unless a longer period is required for legal, tax, security, accounting, or dispute reasons. Emails you send may be kept so we can handle follow-up questions and corrections.

Your choices and rights

You can avoid sending personal information by not emailing us and by not clicking third party links. You can also control cookies in your browser. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to, or restrict certain personal data. To make a privacy request, contact support@uiori.com.

Children

uiori is not intended for children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to uiori, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, corrections, or data requests, contact support@uiori.com.
