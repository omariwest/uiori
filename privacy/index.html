Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information UIORI handles, why it is needed, and how marketplace data is protected and shared.

Last updated 16 November 2026

1Information UIORI handles

UIORI may handle account information such as your name, email address, authentication identifiers, profile information, and account role.

When you use the marketplace, UIORI may also handle campaign content, destination links, selected channels, requested schedules, moderation records, payment and invoice metadata, transaction references, support messages, saved channels, and delivery records.

2Technical and usage information

UIORI may collect device, browser, IP address, page, referral, session, error, and analytics information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and understand the service. Cookies or similar browser storage may be used for authentication, preferences, security, and analytics.

3Payment information

UIORI stores marketplace payment records such as amount, currency, provider status, invoice identifiers, asset, network, transaction reference, and confirmation state. UIORI does not need your wallet seed phrase or private key and will never ask you to provide them.

Crypto transactions may be visible publicly on the relevant blockchain. Payment providers may separately collect information under their own privacy policies. UIORI's current hosted crypto checkout is provided through NOWPayments.

4How information is used

  • Provide accounts, authentication, marketplace browsing, campaigns, billing, moderation, and delivery records.
  • Verify payments and maintain accounting records.
  • Review advertising and prevent fraud, abuse, phishing, payment manipulation, and security incidents.
  • Provide support and communicate about your account or campaigns.
  • Measure and improve UIORI and comply with legal obligations.

5Service providers and sharing

UIORI may share the minimum information needed with service providers that support hosting, database and authentication, analytics, communications, payments, security, and campaign delivery.

Campaign creative and delivery details may be shared with the Telegram channel operator responsible for reviewing or publishing the placement. Public sponsored posts are, by nature, visible to Telegram users.

UIORI may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and security, or as part of a legitimate business transfer. UIORI does not sell personal information to advertisers.

6Authentication providers

If you choose Google or another supported sign in provider, that provider supplies UIORI with the account identifiers and profile information necessary to authenticate you. The provider's own privacy terms also apply to its service.

7Retention

UIORI keeps information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Account information may be deleted or deidentified when no longer needed, but transaction, campaign, moderation, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, or legal records may need to be retained after account deletion.

8Security

UIORI uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information, including access controls and server side handling of privileged credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9International processing

UIORI and its service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for international transfers.

10Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or request a copy of certain personal information. Some records may be retained where required for security, accounting, dispute, or legal purposes.

You may contact UIORI to exercise an applicable privacy right or ask a privacy question.

11Children

UIORI is not intended for children under 18, and the marketplace is designed for adults and businesses purchasing advertising.

12Policy changes and contact

UIORI may update this Privacy Policy as the service, providers, or legal requirements change. The current version and date will be published here.

Privacy questions can be sent through the UIORI Contact page or to support@uiori.com.
