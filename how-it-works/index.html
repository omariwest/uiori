How UIORI works

Start simply. See every step.

Create your account, complete the earning setup, switch it on when you are ready, and track progress from your dashboard.

The process

Four simple steps from signup to rewards.

You do not need to understand the technical side. UIORI keeps the setup and progress easy to follow.

  1. 01

    Create your account

    Your balance, points, streaks, milestones, and withdrawal status stay together.

  2. 02

    Complete the setup

    Follow the guided setup once to connect your browser for earning.

  3. 03

    Turn earning on

    Nothing starts automatically. You choose when earning becomes active.

  4. 04

    Track your progress

    Watch points, milestones, and any credited rewards from your dashboard.

What you will see

The journey stays visible inside your account.

Each stage has a clear place: setup in Earn, progress in Overview, and credited money in Wallet.

01

Set up earning

See your current connection state and the exact next action before anything starts.

02

Watch progress

Your overview shows the credited balance, next milestone, earning state, points, streak, and level.

03

Keep money clear

The Wallet keeps credited earnings and withdrawal status separate from progress scores.

After you start

Your dashboard turns activity into clear progress.

Eligible activity can update your progress. When UIORI credits a reward, it appears separately in your balance.

  1. 01
    Eligible activity is recorded

    Activity that meets UIORI rules can contribute to your progress.

  2. 02
    Progress updates

    Points, streaks, levels, and milestones show how you are moving forward.

  3. 03
    Credited rewards appear in your balance

    Only amounts actually credited by UIORI count as money.

Your control

Earning stays under your control.

  • Creating an account does not start earning.
  • You choose when to turn earning on or off.
  • Points and milestones are progress, not cash.
  • Only credited earnings count toward withdrawals.
