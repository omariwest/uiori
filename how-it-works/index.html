How UIORI works

From account activation to earning — exactly what you will see.

UIORI guides you through the setup once. Review the real screens below before you start, then keep participation on only when you want it.

Start earning How participation works
You stay in control.

The real setup

Eight screens. No guessing.

These are the same screens you will move through when connecting UIORI to Mellowtel.

  1. 01

    Activate your UIORI account

    Open Account settings and complete activation before earning can start.

  2. 02

    Open Earn and start setup

    Go to Earn and choose Set up earning. UIORI shows what the connection means before anything runs.

  3. 03

    Continue to UIORI's Mellowtel invite

    UIORI opens the Mellowtel invite connected to your setup. Add the extension from this page.

  4. 04

    Add the Mellowtel extension

    Confirm Add to Chrome. This installs the browser component used for participation.

  5. 05

    Follow the short demo

    Mellowtel walks you through its controls so you know what the switch does before you continue.

  6. 06

    Finish the demo

    When the walkthrough is complete, finish the setup and return to UIORI.

  7. 07

    Keep support on when you want to participate

    With support on, Mellowtel can use eligible unused bandwidth while you browse.

  8. 08

    Turn it off whenever you want

    Use the same control to stop support. You stay in control of participation.

Your control

Three things stay simple.

  • Creating an account does not start participation.
  • You can turn Mellowtel support on or off at any time.
  • Points and milestones show progress; only credited earnings are cash.
