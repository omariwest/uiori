How UIORI works

Start simply. See every step.

Create your account, complete earning setup on a desktop, and track your progress.

The process

Eight simple steps from signup to earning.

These screens show each step from signup to earning. Follow them in order and you will always know what comes next.

1

Create or sign in to your account

Use your UIORI account to continue to earning setup.

2

Open Earn and start setup

Choose Set up earning. UIORI explains what participation uses before anything opens.

3

Open the support page

UIORI takes you to its Mellowtel support page. Review it, then continue with Add for free.

4

Add the extension

For example, on the Chrome Web Store, choose Add to Chrome to install the Mellowtel extension.

5

Go through the short demo

Mellowtel briefly shows how support works and where its controls are. Continue with Next Step.

6

Complete the demo

Finish the Mellowtel demo, then return to UIORI. The extension is verified automatically in this browser.

7

Turn support on

When you choose to participate, turn support on. A small part of your unused internet connection can then be used while you browse.

8

Turn it off anytime

Open Mellowtel settings whenever you want to stop support. The control remains yours.

After you start

Your dashboard turns activity into clear progress.

Eligible activity can update your progress. When UIORI credits a reward, it appears separately in your balance.

  1. 1
    Eligible activity is recorded

    Activity that meets UIORI rules can contribute to your progress.

  2. 2
    Progress updates

    Points, streaks, levels, and milestones show how you are moving forward.

  3. 3
    Credited rewards appear in your balance

    Only amounts actually credited by UIORI count as money.

Your control

Earning stays under your control.

  • Creating an account does not start earning.
  • You choose when to turn earning on or off.
  • Points and milestones track your progress.
  • Only credited earnings count toward withdrawals.
