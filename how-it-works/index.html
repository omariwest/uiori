How it works

From audience search to delivery proof.

UIORI keeps the buying flow simple: choose inventory, submit the creative, confirm the terms, fund an approved campaign, and track what was delivered.

1Find channels

Browse available Telegram inventory and compare the factual information UIORI has for each channel. Missing metrics or prices are not replaced with invented values.

2Create the campaign

Select one or more eligible channels, add the sponsored-post creative and destination, and choose a preferred schedule. A requested time is not confirmed until UIORI reviews availability.

3Review and fund

UIORI reviews the creative against the Advertising Policy. Approved campaigns receive a confirmed campaign total and can then be funded through the supported checkout. An invoice by itself does not make a campaign funded.

4Publish and verify

After funding and scheduling, UIORI publishes the approved placement and records delivery evidence. Standard placements follow their confirmed time terms. Eligible reach-backed products use Telegram-reported post views within the stated delivery window.

5If delivery misses the agreed terms

UIORI applies the published delivery-protection rules. Depending on the order, the remedy may be correction, rescheduling, a make-good placement, credit, or refund of the undelivered value.

6What UIORI does not guarantee

Unless a specific order says otherwise, UIORI does not guarantee clicks, leads, conversions, sales, subscriber growth, token performance, or verified unique humans. Reach-backed products refer to Telegram-reported post views.
