Refund & Delivery Protection Policy

Clear rules for unpaid invoices, funded campaigns, standard placements, reach-backed delivery, credits, and refunds.

Last updated 16 November 2026

1What this policy covers

This policy explains cancellation, underdelivery, make good, credit, and refund handling for UIORI advertising campaigns. It should be read together with the Terms and the specific delivery terms shown for an order.

2Before funding

Draft and approved campaigns that have not been funded do not create a paid delivery obligation. An unpaid or expired invoice does not make a campaign funded.

3Payment confirmation

Crypto payments may pass through waiting, confirming, partial, expired, failed, or similar provider states. UIORI treats a campaign as funded only after the required final payment confirmation is received.

If an invoice is underpaid, overpaid, duplicated, expired, or paid after expiry, UIORI may require manual reconciliation before the campaign can proceed. Do not send a second payment unless UIORI or the checkout provider instructs you to do so.

4Cancellation before publication

If you request cancellation after funding but before publication, UIORI will review whether inventory or other nonrecoverable costs have already been committed. Where cancellation is accepted, the unused campaign value may be returned as credit or refunded. Unavoidable blockchain, processor, exchange, or third party fees may not be recoverable.

5If UIORI cannot deliver

If UIORI cannot provide an agreed placement for reasons not caused by the advertiser, UIORI may offer a replacement placement, rescheduling, account credit, or a refund of the undelivered campaign value.

UIORI will not substitute materially different inventory without advertiser approval.

6Standard placements

For a standard time based placement, low views alone do not create a refund right unless a view floor was explicitly included in the order. UIORI does not guarantee clicks, conversions, sales, subscriber growth, or other advertiser outcomes.

If the post is published incorrectly, removed before the agreed minimum period, or materially fails the confirmed placement terms, UIORI will first try to correct the delivery. If it cannot be corrected, a make good, credit, or proportional refund may apply.

7Reach backed placements

Where an order explicitly includes a reach guarantee, the contracted metric is the Telegram reported post view counter. It is not a guarantee of unique people.

Unless the order states different terms, UIORI's initial model uses a minimum live period of 6 hours and a maximum delivery window of 72 hours. If the contracted view floor is not reached by the end of the delivery window, UIORI may extend once, provide a make good on eligible inventory, issue credit, or refund the undelivered portion.

Natural overdelivery is included at no additional charge.

8Advertiser caused problems

No delivery protection applies to losses caused by the advertiser materially changing or disabling the destination, providing unlawful or deceptive content, losing access to required assets, or otherwise preventing approved delivery.

If UIORI must reject or stop a funded campaign for a policy violation, unused campaign value will normally be returned after deducting nonrecoverable third party costs, unless law, fraud prevention, sanctions, or a security investigation requires a different treatment.

9How refunds are sent

Crypto payments are generally irreversible at the network level. When a monetary refund is approved, UIORI may need to create a separate outbound transaction. UIORI may require confirmation of the destination address, asset, and network before sending it.

UIORI may use account credit instead of an on chain refund only when the advertiser agrees or where the original order terms explicitly provide for credit.

10Requesting review

For cancellation, delivery, or refund questions, contact support@uiori.com or use the Contact page and include the campaign or payment reference. UIORI will review the campaign record, payment state, publication evidence, and applicable delivery terms.
