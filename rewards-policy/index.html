Rewards Policy

This policy explains the difference between UIORI progress, credited earnings, and withdrawal eligibility.

Last updated October 2026

1 The reward system

UIORI operates its own participation-based reward system. An account may display Contribution Points, streaks, levels, milestones, and credited earnings.

These elements do not all represent money.

2 Points, streaks, levels, and milestones

Contribution Points are a server-controlled measure of eligible participation and progress. Streaks, levels, and milestones help make that progress easier to follow.

None of these indicators is automatically a cash balance, and UIORI does not promise a fixed monetary value for a Contribution Point.

3 Credited earnings

Credited earnings are monetary amounts that UIORI has actually awarded to an account under the applicable reward rules.

Only amounts recorded as credited earnings form part of the user's UIORI monetary balance and count toward withdrawal eligibility.

4 How rewards are determined

UIORI may determine rewards using eligible participation, Contribution Points, account status, the reward rules in effect, and available reward funding generated through legitimate platform participation.

UIORI does not publish a fixed reward formula in this policy.

5 No fixed earning rate

UIORI does not guarantee a fixed hourly or daily earning rate, a fixed value per Contribution Point, a particular income amount, or a specific time required to reach a milestone.

UIORI is not an investment product.

6 Eligible participation and limits

Only participation considered valid under UIORI's platform rules may contribute toward rewards.

UIORI may apply reasonable limits to points, progress, or reward-generating activity to prevent manipulation, repeated refreshing or clicking, automated farming, or other activity inconsistent with the reward system.

7 Reward corrections

UIORI may correct points, progress, or credited earnings where there is evidence of duplicate credits, invalid activity, manipulation, technical or accounting error, or system malfunction.

Corrections are intended to maintain accurate balances and reward integrity.

8 Withdrawals

Only credited earnings count toward withdrawal eligibility. UIORI's current minimum withdrawal is $5.00. The account must be active, the email address must be verified, and the account must be at least seven days old.

A payout destination must be configured before a withdrawal can be requested. New or changed payout destinations have a 24-hour safety period. UIORI currently supports USDT payouts on BNB Smart Chain (BEP20), permits one open withdrawal request at a time, and processes approved requests in a monthly payout batch.

Withdrawal requests may be reviewed where account or activity verification is needed. Requests associated with suspected manipulation, invalid activity, technical error, or a violation of platform rules may be delayed, adjusted, or rejected.

9 Changes to reward rules

UIORI's reward mechanics may evolve. Changes may affect future point accumulation, levels, milestones, earning calculations, or withdrawal requirements.

Material changes should be reflected in UIORI's published policies or product information.

10 Mellowtel

Bandwidth-sharing participation is provided through Mellowtel's separate companion-extension flow. UIORI operates its own reward system and does not claim to reproduce Mellowtel's exact internal per-user accounting.

11 Questions

Questions about UIORI rewards can be submitted through the UIORI Contact page or sent to support@uiori.com.
