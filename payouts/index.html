Payout methods

Start with the method you can actually receive.

A platform is only useful if its payout method works for your country, account, fees, and verification situation.

PayPalStrong when receiving is supportedUseful for survey, testing, and offer platforms, but receiving ability and withdrawal options vary sharply by country.CryptoFlexible but higher riskCrypto can be useful for low-payout sites and offerwalls, but fees, volatility, account rules, and local regulations matter.PayoneerUseful for work platformsPayoneer is often more relevant for freelance, contractor, and project-based platforms than tiny survey apps.Gift CardsGood for small rewardsGift cards can be easier than cash in some countries, but resale value and regional restrictions matter.Bank TransferBest when supported directlyBank transfer is clean when available, but many small earning platforms do not support it in every country.
