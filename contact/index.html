Before you email

Do not send passwords, government IDs, wallet seed phrases, private keys, payment card numbers, or login codes. uiori will never need those details to review a correction.

Best report format

Clear reports help us update country pages, payout notes, and platform warnings faster.

Include Example Why it helps
Country Egypt, India, Nigeria, Morocco Platform availability changes by location.
Platform Clickworker, ySense, TimeBucks, Freecash We can update the correct page.
Payout method PayPal, Payoneer, crypto, gift card, bank transfer Payout options often differ by country.
What changed Minimum withdrawal changed, account rejected, payout option removed. Specific reports are easier to verify.
Date checked May 2026 Old MMO information becomes risky quickly.

Good reasons to contact us

  • A platform no longer works in your country.
  • A payout method, minimum withdrawal, or fee appears outdated.
  • A page needs a stronger warning or clearer wording.
  • You found a broken link, typo, or incorrect internal link.
  • You have a sponsored listing, partnership, or media request.
  • You have a privacy, terms, or affiliate disclosure question.

What uiori cannot do

uiori cannot approve third-party accounts, guarantee task access, reverse bans, recover passwords, process withdrawals, verify your identity for another platform, or promise that a listed platform will work for your profile.
