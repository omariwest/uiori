About UIORI

Telegram advertising without the admin chase.

UIORI is building a cleaner marketplace for advertisers to compare Telegram inventory, create campaigns, fund approved orders, and track delivery in one place.

1What UIORI is

UIORI is an independent Telegram advertising marketplace. Telegram provides the distribution infrastructure; UIORI provides the commercial layer around channel discovery, campaign review, funding, scheduling, delivery proof, and reporting.

2What advertisers should be able to judge

UIORI is designed around the signals that matter before buying: audience relevance, recent reach, placement terms, price, moderation rules, and delivery evidence. Metrics are shown only when the underlying data exists.

3Why start with owned channels

UIORI's first inventory may be operated by UIORI itself. That gives the marketplace real channels to learn from before opening broader channel-partner onboarding. The long-term product is a curated multi-owner marketplace, not a closed media network.

4Trust before scale

UIORI does not need thousands of listings on day one. It needs inventory with understandable metrics, clear delivery terms, responsible advertising review, and honest protection when an agreed placement cannot be delivered.

5Independent from Telegram

UIORI is not Telegram and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Telegram unless explicitly stated otherwise.
