About

Useful earning guidance first. Monetization second.

uiori helps users compare country availability, task types, payout methods, minimum withdrawal notes, warnings, and first-payout routes.

What we do

We organize third-party earning platforms by country, payout method, and task type. Our goal is to help users avoid unsupported platforms, fake income claims, and high-threshold traps.

What we do not do

We do not employ users, process payouts, run a wallet, guarantee earnings, or ask users to complete tasks for us in exchange for fake points.

Core rule

Users earn from third-party platforms if they qualify. uiori only helps them research routes more safely.
