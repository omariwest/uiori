About UIORI

Small rewards. Clear progress. No hype.

UIORI is built to make earning easy to follow: what you have earned, how your progress is moving, and what comes next.

Why we are building UIORI

Online earning can become confusing when points, activity, balances, and promises are mixed together.

UIORI keeps it simple: progress stays visible, credited earnings stay clear, and you choose when earning is active.

What the product is trying to do well

  • Make earning easy to start and understand.
  • Show progress without pretending every indicator is money.
  • Keep credited earnings easy to identify.
  • Make withdrawal eligibility easy to follow.

What you see in UIORI

  • Your account and earning status
  • Contribution Points, streaks, levels, and milestones
  • Credited earnings and next goals
  • Withdrawal status and eligibility

One place for the full journey

UIORI brings setup, progress, your credited balance, and withdrawals into one consistent experience.

What matters to us

A small set of product principles.

Clarity

You should be able to tell the difference between activity, progress, and actual credited earnings.

Control

Earning should be a deliberate choice, with clear status and simple controls.

Sustainability

Rewards should stay realistic and sustainable, without inflated promises or artificial balances.
